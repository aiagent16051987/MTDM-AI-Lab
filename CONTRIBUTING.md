# Contributing

Contributions may be proposed through GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.

- Submit work you created or are authorized to contribute.
- Identify third-party code, data, models, images and documentation with their sources and applicable licences.
- Do not submit secrets, personal data or confidential material.
- Include relevant tests and factual evidence for technical changes.
- Significant changes may be reviewed or declined by the maintainer.
