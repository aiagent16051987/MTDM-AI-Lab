Swarm Robotics Simulator

Use one slider at a time and compare clustering, heading alignment and group dispersion.

MODEL NOTESEDUCATIONAL

Three interaction rules

  • Separation: steer away from nearby agents.
  • Alignment: match neighbours' average heading.
  • Cohesion: move toward the local group centre.

Limitations

This is a 2D kinematic demonstration, not a validated robot-fleet controller. It omits robot dimensions, obstacles, communication loss, localization error, actuator dynamics, collision certification and real hardware.

Suggested experiments

  1. Compare group behaviour at different agent counts.
  2. Study how separation changes clustering.
  3. Find parameter combinations that cause fragmentation.
  4. Plan a future obstacle-avoidance benchmark.
SafetySimulation only. Any future physical robotics feature requires supervised testing, independent safety controls and a separate review.

PROCESS SELECTION GUIDE

Major 3D Printing Technologies

Learn what each process does, which materials it handles and where it is useful.

Material extrusionFDM / FFF, pellet extrusion

Thermoplastic filament or pellets are melted and deposited layer by layer. Common for prototypes, fixtures and functional polymer parts.

Vat photopolymerizationSLA, DLP, MSLA / LCD

Liquid photopolymer is selectively cured by laser, projector or masked light. High detail; resin handling and post-cure controls matter.

Powder bed fusion · polymersSLS, MJF

Polymer powder is fused selectively by laser or agent-assisted thermal process. Supports complex parts and production batches.

Powder bed fusion · metalsLPBF / SLM / DMLS, EB-PBF / EBM

Metal powder is fused using laser or electron beam. Requires qualified equipment, inert/vacuum process controls and specialist post-processing.

Binder jettingMetal, sand, ceramic powders

A liquid binder joins powder particles; parts commonly need curing, depowdering, sintering or infiltration.

Material jettingPhotopolymers, wax-like materials

Droplets are deposited and solidified, enabling fine features and multi-material colour or property combinations on suitable machines.

Directed energy depositionDED, LMD, laser / electron / arc

Feedstock is delivered into a melt pool for repair, cladding and larger metal features. Process planning and metallurgy are demanding.

Wire arc additive manufacturingWAAM

Electric arc melts wire feedstock to build large metal structures. Useful for large near-net-shape components with machining allowance.

Sheet laminationLOM, ultrasonic additive manufacturing

Sheets or foils are bonded and shaped into a 3D object. Bond quality and finishing depend on the specific process.

Construction printingConcrete / mortar extrusion

Pumpable cementitious material is deposited along toolpaths. Mix rheology, curing, reinforcement and structural engineering are essential.

Bioprinting · researchHydrogel / cell-laden bioinks

Research overview only. Biological viability, sterility and medical regulation make this fundamentally different from ordinary desktop printing.

Hybrid additive manufacturingAdd + CNC / subtractive finishing

Combines additive deposition with machining, inspection and finishing to achieve precision surfaces and interfaces.
