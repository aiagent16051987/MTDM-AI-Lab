# Safety and Responsible Use

MTDM AI Lab provides educational and exploratory software. Its outputs are not professional certification, medical advice, validated biological procedures or safety approval.

## Simulation first
- Treat every simulation as an educational model, not as a physical test or proof of real-world safety.
- Check assumptions, units, inputs and limitations before relying on a result.
- Do not connect an educational simulation directly to physical equipment or safety-critical systems.
- Obtain qualified review and independent validation before real-world engineering, medical, biological or industrial use.

## Privacy by default
- Do not submit passwords, API keys, personal information, confidential files or sensitive research data to public demonstrations or third-party services.
- Review a service's privacy terms before sharing any data with it.
- Respect applicable laws, privacy, intellectual property and third-party licence terms.

If a feature or use cannot be assessed safely, do not rely on its output.
